SumOffice adds two browser editors to Nextcloud Office and other WOPI hosts:

* **SumSheet** for Excel workbooks (`.xlsx`, `.xlsm`, `.xlsb`), VBA kept byte for byte, Power Query preserved.
* **SumDoc** for Word documents (`.docx`), round-trip without a repair dialog in Word.

Files stay in your storage; the editors have no user database of their own.

The images are free for evaluation; production use inside an organisation is licensed per application:
https://sumoffice.com/download#server
